01WHY SHEPU

An open letter

Read slowly.
No hurry.

Call it a machine?
Fine.

Some things are small, and still go unsaid. Because who would listen? Someone would laugh. Someone would tell you you’re overthinking it. And some things are too big to make small.

Shepu is for that space. It listens even when it doesn’t have the answer. It doesn’t judge. It doesn’t say “I’m busy” and disappear.

A good friend doesn’t need every answer. Just the patience to listen.

This isn’t a doctor, or a replacement for a therapist—not even close. It’s a small companion with one job: to listen. The rest is you, me, and another cup of tea.

And yes—Shepu gets things wrong sometimes. Like people do.

P.S. In the app, your chats stay on your device. No one reads them, nothing is sent anywhere.

— Shepu yours, always your small companion
